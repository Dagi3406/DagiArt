About The Artist

Turning feelings into images

I'm Dagim Gebeyehu, a painter and illustrator. Every piece starts as a feeling or a story, and I work to translate it into something you can see — from quiet pencil sketches to expressive portraits and concept art.

Whether you want a portrait of someone you love or a concept for a world of your own, I'd love to hear about it.

How I Work

  1. 01

    Share Your Idea

    Tell me about the subject, mood and size you have in mind. References are always welcome.

  2. 02

    First Sketch

    I draw a rough concept so we can agree on composition and direction early.

  3. 03

    Refine & Render

    Details, light and texture come together until the piece feels alive.

  4. 04

    Final Delivery

    You review the finished artwork and receive it ready to frame, print or share.
